Project description

Infinite Jest is a The Sims machinima series based on David Foster Wallace’s novel. The book’s structure—resembling a Sierpiński triangle and a game of professional tennis—is translated into fragmented videos designed for nonlinear viewing. Any clip can be a starting point; the beginning contains the end, the end folds into the beginning, but they don’t necessarily belong to the same story.

While Infinite Jest has a narrative, it emerges from overlapping storylines. Machinima, as a medium, always engages with the internal structures of the game it’s created in. In this case, The Sims offers a more fitting way to reimagine the novel’s themes than a conventional adaptation.

The 25-year history of The Sims—its lore, mechanics, and linguistic and behavioral clichés—unexpectedly aligns with Wallace’s use of repetition and nonverbal functions of clichés. Most of the machinima is filmed in The Sims 4, a game often criticized by its community, which I associate with the novel’s Subsidized Time era. Events set before Subsidized Time are recreated in earlier The Sims installments.

The series employs different filming techniques and approaches to character interactions, from recreating game conditions described in the novel to observing unscripted interactions between a character and players in The Sims Online (FreeSO).
